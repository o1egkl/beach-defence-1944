Bunker Combat Manual
• Aim & Fire: Move Mouse (or Drag on touch) to aim the 3D Machine Gun; hold Left-Click or Space to fire tracer bursts.
• Limited Shots & Cooldown: When your ammo belt runs out, the barrel overheats for a few seconds. Press [R] to cool/reload early during lulls.

• Grenades & Repair Supplies: Press [G] / Right-Click to lob an anti-armor grenade; shoot floating Parachute Crates (+10% Health) or press [T] for Emergency Repair Kits (+25% Health) to maintain Bunker Integrity. PACIFIC DEFENCE — 3D GAME PLAN

1. 3D First-Person Setting & Engine

Built on Three.js (WebGL) from a fortified coastal pillbox overlooking an amphibious invasion beach. Features animated vertex waves, sand obstacles (Czech hedgehogs, wire, craters), and a 3D mounted Heavy Machine Gun with recoil, muzzle flash, tracers, and overheat steam.

2. Limited Shots & Gun Cooldown System

The player starts with a 45-round belt. Expending the belt triggers a mandatory multi-second Barrel Cooldown (2.6s base) before firing resumes. Upgrades expand belt capacity and install water-cooled jackets to cut cooldown time.

3. 20-Wave Campaign & Multi-Tier Armor

  • Waves 1–4 (3 mins each): Recon Boats, Infantry Assault Squads & Armored Scout Cars.
  • Waves 5–9 (3 mins each): Medium Armor Half-Tracks & First Heavy Amphibious Tank Columns.
  • Waves 10–19 (3 mins each): Full Amphibious Armored Divisions with Escalating Heavy Amphibious Tanks.
  • Wave 20 (6-min Final Boss): Operation Titan Dreadnought — 6-Minute Siege against the ★ Supreme Armada Flagship Colossus ★!

4. Score Formula, Upgrades, Grenades & Time

  • Score Formula: Combines Remaining Time + Shot Accuracy % + Time × Accuracy Synergy.
  • Progressive Upgrades: Available before every level; higher waves unlock stronger upgrade tiers and higher per-tier stat scaling.
  • Bunker Health & Consumables: Shoot floating Parachute Crates to restore Bunker Health (+10%). Use key T for emergency repair kits (+25% Health). Enemies that reach the bunker perimeter without being killed diminish health (10% basic rifleman, up to 35% tank, 50% dreadnought). Round ends in defeat if received damage reaches 100%! Plus +1 Grenade on every One-Shot Kill!
