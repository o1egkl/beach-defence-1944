## 🎖️ Pacific Defence v1.0.0 — Official Windows Release

**Pacific Defence** is a high-octane 3D first-person coastal bunker defense arcade game built with Three.js and packaged as a native desktop application with Electron.

Hold the coastal fortress against incoming waves of amphibious landing craft, assault stormtroopers, mechanized armor, and naval bombardment fleets!

### 🌟 Key Highlights & Gameplay Features

- **Panoramic Bunker Viewport:** Precision-engineered low concrete parapet shelf (~11% bottom lip) providing **~88% unobstructed battlefield visibility** across sand dunes, water shoreline, and beach obstacles.
- **4-Weapon Tactical Arsenal:**
  - **Rapid MG (Slot 1):** Sustained suppressing fire with barrel heating, real-time cooling gauge, and animated recoil.
  - **75mm AT Cannon (Slot 2):** High-velocity armor-piercing kinetic rounds for eliminating landing craft and amphibious tanks.
  - **Heavy AT Rocket Launcher (Slot 3):** High-explosive shaped-charge warheads with massive blast radii.
  - **Fragmentation Grenades ([G] / RMB):** Heavy lobbed explosives to clear clustered infantry squads.
- **Tactical Overdrive ([T]):** Emergency adrenaline bullet-time slow-motion + field repair + instant weapon vent and ammo refill.
- **Command Bunker & Weapon Upgrade Armory:** Spend requisition score between waves to upgrade fire rate, AP penetration, reload speeds, and max capacity across all weapons.
- **Procedural 3D Ocean Simulation:** Multi-speed waves, shore shoaling, breaking surf, and dynamic foam trails.
- **Synthetic Battlefield Audio:** 100% procedural Web Audio synthesizer with sound effects for gunfire, explosions, mechanical reload clanks, and ocean ambience.
- **20-Wave Campaign Progression:** From light recon patrols up to heavy dreadnought invasion fleets.
